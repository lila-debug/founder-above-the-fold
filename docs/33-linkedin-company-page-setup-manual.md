### Safety Stickers
- [Security] Keep passwords, cookies, access keys, browser storage and account recovery details out of frame.
- [Privacy] Hide LinkedIn messages, notifications, personal contact details and any street address.
- [Cost] Do not buy ads, boosts or additional Premium features as part of this setup.
- [IP] Show outcomes, public copy and public surfaces; conceal prompts, code, architecture and unpublished mechanisms.
- [Evidence] Do not call the Page converted, launch-ready or revenue-producing until visits, qualified actions and paid outcomes exist.
- [LinkedIn] Company-page edits and final saves remain human actions; no scraping or automated profile editing.
