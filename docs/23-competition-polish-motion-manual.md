### Assembly Steps
#### Step 1 - Lift the first product panel
Diagram:
```text
[Compact header] ---> [Compact stage rail] ---> [Visible active panel]
```
Do:
1. Shorten the header and stage rails without crowding their labels.
2. Place the active panel inside the first judge viewport.
3. Clamp poster headings to a readable width and controlled scale.
4. Preserve the fourteen-screen selector, current-step label and manual panel.

Check:
- All fourteen screens pass at 1440x900, 390x844 and 2048x1152.
- No inspected screen has horizontal overflow, clipped poster type, a missing first panel or a browser-console error.

Avoid:
- Shrinking labels below a useful reading size just to win vertical space.
- Hiding setup or safety states to make the cabinet look more complete than it is.

#### Step 2 - Fasten the presenter motion
Diagram:
```text
[Untouched source deck] ---> [Transition jig] ---> [Animated presenter copy]
```
Do:
1. Leave the source PowerPoint panel untouched.
2. Insert restrained fade, push and wipe transitions into a separate copy.
3. Keep click-advance control with the presenter.
4. Open and render the finished copy with LibreOffice as an independent inspection.

Check:
- Thirteen slides remain present.
- Thirteen native transition records are fitted.
- PowerPoint package validation passes against the source.

Avoid:
- Auto-advancing a live pitch deck.
- Adding motion that competes with the founder's spoken case.

#### Step 3 - Assemble the launch-film story
Diagram:
```text
[Second-job pain] -> [Name the machine] -> [Show proof] -> [Clamp safety] -> [Five-build offer]
```
Do:
1. Open with the unwanted LinkedIn second job.
2. Reject generic schedulers and engagement bots.
3. Lock the Founder Above the Fold category plate into view.
4. Route the magic link, profile parts, voice check and two-week queue through labelled bays.
5. Clamp official publishing, no scraping, no fake engagement and no risky profile automation to the safety rail.
6. Close on the private beta and five founder builds.

Check:
- Runtime, layout, motion and all 45 contrast inspections pass.
- Seven readable poster frames exist at the final beat of every scene.
- All seven narration clips fit their labelled scene slots without overlap or clipping.
- The final file decodes completely, contains all 1,350 frames and has no detected black gap of 100 ms or longer.
- The 55 caption groups reproduce all 116 approved source tokens verbatim.

Avoid:
- Fake LinkedIn screens, invented analytics, customer logos or fabricated outcomes.
- Changing the locked spoken rail without rebuilding both the narration and caption tracks.
