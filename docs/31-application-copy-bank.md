## Part G - Accelerator Application Copy

### Company one-liner

Prototype Cafe builds owner-controlled AI products that turn confusing workflows into clear, working systems.

### What does your company do?

Prototype Cafe identifies high-friction workflows that are important, repetitive and poorly served, then turns them into owner-controlled AI products. Each product combines working software, agentic assistance, strong visual systems and an interactive operating manual. We launched three products in four weeks and are now using customer evidence to determine which product earns concentrated company resources.

### What problem are you solving?

Powerful digital workflows are increasingly difficult to understand and trust. Users face scattered tools, invisible automation, confusing setup and unclear boundaries between what the machine does and what remains their responsibility. Prototype Cafe makes those mechanisms visible, testable and controllable.

For Founder Above the Fold specifically, the problem is that founder LinkedIn credibility matters, but maintaining profile truth and consistent content becomes a second job. Existing tools often add risky automation instead of reducing the cognitive and operational burden.

### What is your solution?

We build products as visible operating systems. Founder Above the Fold combines versioned profile copy, an exact-revision voice clamp, a controlled content queue, official-API publishing rails, owner-post analytics, MCP tools and an interactive manual. Users can see what is fitted, what is blocked, what remains human and what evidence is required before a capability is called ready.

### Why now?

AI has dramatically reduced the cost of producing software and content, but it has increased the need for judgement, control and legibility. More activity is easy. Trustworthy systems that preserve human authority are not. Prototype Cafe is building for that gap.

### What is your traction?

In the past four weeks, I launched three products through Prototype Cafe. Founder Above the Fold has a live public demonstration, a working web and MCP product, a tested interactive manual, launch media and repeatable browser and build checks. This is execution evidence, not yet a claim of product-market fit. The next traction milestones are qualified users, repeated weekly use, paid conversion and retention.

### Why will you win?

Our advantage is the integration of product judgement, engineering, visual communication and operational safety. We do not bolt instructions onto finished software. We build the product and its interactive manual together, making complicated systems easier to understand, test and trust. The launch of three products in four weeks also shows that we can compress the path from idea to working evidence.

### What is the business model?

The commercial model is staged. Founder Above the Fold begins with limited founder-led setup and visibility operations, expands into self-serve software, and supports an outright macOS purchase for customers who prefer ownership over another subscription. Pricing, conversion and the final revenue mix remain validation questions until live purchase and customer evidence are collected.

### Who is the customer?

The initial customer is a founder or fractional product leader whose credibility and pipeline depend partly on LinkedIn, but who dislikes content administration and rejects scraping, automated outreach and fake engagement. The broader Prototype Cafe customer is a professional who needs a powerful AI-assisted workflow without surrendering visibility or control.

### What is your unfair advantage?

The founder can move across product strategy, interaction design, technical implementation, testing, narrative and launch without waiting for a chain of specialist hand-offs. The interactive manual system also creates a reusable product language that can travel across the portfolio.

### What have you learned?

Building quickly is no longer the scarce part. Choosing the correct problem, preserving trust and gathering real behavioural evidence are the hard parts. Launching three products in four weeks proved the assembly system; the next discipline is concentrating on the product that earns repeated use and payment.

### What will you accomplish during the programme?

We will convert launch velocity into commercial evidence. The goals are to interview and onboard qualified users, measure repeated use, validate willingness to pay, identify the strongest product in the portfolio, concentrate engineering and distribution on that winner, and leave the programme with a tested acquisition and retention loop rather than a larger collection of features.

### Long-term vision

Prototype Cafe will become a portfolio company for owner-controlled AI products: software that makes complicated work clearer, safer and more desirable while preserving human judgement. The common layer is a reusable system for agents, evidence, permissions and interactive visual manuals.

### What are you applying for?

We are looking for disciplined customer access, commercial pressure-testing and founder-level guidance on portfolio concentration, pricing, distribution and capital strategy. The product-building engine is working. The immediate need is to turn that velocity into repeated customer value and a focused, defensible company.

### Safety Stickers
- [Evidence] Three launches prove execution speed, not market fit.
- [Revenue] Do not describe a planned business model as current revenue.
- [Customer] Do not convert testers, visitors or waitlist names into active users.
- [Team] Do not imply a larger team than exists.
- [Market] Do not invent TAM, growth rates or market share; verify them for any programme that requires market sizing.
- [IP] Use the exact registered or pending status approved by the owner; do not expand trademark or patent claims.
