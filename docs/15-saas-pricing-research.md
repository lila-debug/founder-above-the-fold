### Recommended Price Jig
**Primary public offer:** CA$7,500 once.

**Best introductory offer:** keep the full CA$7,500 price and limit the first public allocation to five founder builds. Do not discount. The introduction is scarcity of delivery capacity, not a coupon.

**What the buyer gets:** founder positioning, above-the-fold profile assembly kit, headline/About/Experience/Featured copy, founder signal map, owner voice guide, ten launch-ready posts, private web and Mac operating workbench, safety manual, handover and one bounded revision round.

**What the buyer does not get:** profile editing by the app, scraping, auto-DMs, auto-connects, auto-likes, auto-comments, follower buying, benchmark claims from unavailable data, or any guarantee of LinkedIn reach.

### Assembly Steps
#### Step 1 - Remove the bargain shelf
Diagram:
```text
[Low monthly SaaS] --X--> [Founder transformation]
```
Do:
1. Keep CA$7,500 as the visible price.
2. Describe the offer as a done-with-you credibility build plus owned operating cabinet.
3. Label the introductory mechanism as five founder builds, not a discount.

Check:
- The page cannot be mistaken for a CA$199 licence, a profile gig, or a cheap scheduler.

Avoid:
- Any line that says or implies unlimited support, lifetime hosting, guaranteed reach, or automated LinkedIn growth.

#### Step 2 - Fasten the premium proof points
Diagram:
```text
[Profile source of truth] + [Voice lock] + [Official API posting] + [Manual handoff]
        |
        v
[Premium compliance-led founder cabinet]
```
Do:
1. Place the official-API safety boundary beside the price.
2. Show the manual-only profile copy step as a feature, not a weakness.
3. Make the private Mac/web cabinet part of the value, not an afterthought.

Check:
- A buyer can see why the offer differs from Taplio-style SaaS and Fiverr-style profile services.

Avoid:
- Comparing Founder Above the Fold as if it were just a scheduler.

#### Step 3 - Test the offer without cheapening it
Diagram:
```text
[20 qualified founders] ---> [CA$7,500 page] ---> [Call / checkout / decline reason]
```
Do:
1. Send the page only to qualified founders who have a real profile-positioning problem.
2. Track calls booked, checkout starts, completed payments, refunds, support time and first decline reason.
3. Interview non-buyers on whether the price felt unclear, too low to trust, too high for the promise, or mismatched to their problem.

Check:
- Keep the price if at least two of twenty qualified founders either buy, request invoice/payment timing, or ask for a serious sales call without asking for a discount.

Avoid:
- Measuring unqualified Product Hunt traffic as pricing evidence.

### Safety Stickers
- [Security] Never expose Stripe keys, webhook secrets, licence signing keys, LinkedIn tokens or purchaser records in public panels.
- [Privacy] The buyer must understand what data is collected before checkout and before profile/content work begins.
- [LinkedIn] Do not automate profile edits, scraping, DMs, follows, likes, comments or reposts.
- [Cost] Do not include unlimited hosting, unlimited AI usage or indefinite support inside a one-time price.
- [Evidence] CA$7,500 is the selected premium offer and a rational test. It is not yet statistically proven by completed sales.
