### Assembly Steps
#### Step 1 - Set the watch
Diagram:
```text
[Launch opens] ---> [Maker comment visible] ---> [Reply windows] ---> [End-of-day audit]
```

Do:
1. Schedule only after the launch gate is green. Product Hunt's general planning rule is 12:01 a.m. Pacific; in Toronto during daylight-saving time that is 3:01 a.m. Eastern. Choose a later time if the founder cannot remain present—Product Hunt's current guidance says readiness and authentic engagement matter more than timing folklore.
2. Inspect at launch, start of the founder's working day, midday, mid-afternoon, early evening, and before the Product Hunt day closes.
3. Reply to every substantive comment manually.

Check:
- The maker comment is present.
- No response waits so long that a question appears abandoned.

Avoid:
- Bots, canned mass replies, or asking anyone to upvote.

#### Step 2 - Classify each incoming part
Diagram:
```text
[Comment] ---> [Question | Objection | Bug | Praise | Comparison | Access request]
```

Route:
- Question: answer directly, then link the relevant product mechanism if helpful.
- Objection: name the trade-off honestly; do not argue with the person.
- Bug: acknowledge, reproduce, label impact, and give a truthful next update.
- Praise: thank them and ask what use case made the value clear.
- Comparison: explain the safety/manual difference without attacking competitors.
- Access request: send them to the public route or consented beta intake; do not collect emails in comments.

#### Step 3 - Use answer jigs

Safety question:
> Founder Above the Fold never needs your LinkedIn password and does not scrape profiles or automate DMs, connections, likes, or comments. Publishing uses LinkedIn's official API rail only after the owner connects it. Which part of the permission boundary would you want made more visible?

“Why not use a scheduler?”:
> A scheduler solves the timer. This product also keeps approved profile positioning versioned, locks each draft revision behind a voice check, and ships the operating manual beside the workflow. The useful comparison is whether those controls remove enough recurring founder admin to justify a separate tool.

AI/voice question:
> Voice capture starts only after an owner action. The transcript is editable, and the recording is removed after the request; the transcript is retained only when the owner explicitly keeps it. The public mechanism demo does not upload anything.

Bug response:
> Thank you—this is a real breakpoint. I have classified it as **[app/manual/expectation/context/access]**, reproduced **[what happened]**, and the safe workaround is **[workaround or “none yet”]**. I will update this thread when the fix is verified.

Pricing question:
> Founder Above the Fold has one proposed Canadian offer, internally labelled `founder_transformation`: a CA$7,500 one-time founder transformation. It combines positioning, assembled profile assets, a signal map and voice guide, ten launch posts, handover, private workbench access and one owned Mac licence. It becomes available only after the matching payment, cancellation, refund, dispute, support, privacy and access paths pass their live test.

Competitor comparison:
> The difference is not “more automation.” It is owner control: canonical profile copy, an exact-revision voice clamp, official-API-only publishing, and a visual manual with finished-build tests. If you tell me which tool you use now, I can answer the overlap precisely.

#### Step 4 - Fasten feedback to the product
Diagram:
```text
[Public comment] ---> [Evidence note] ---> [Reproduce] ---> [Product/manual fix] ---> [Public update]
```

Record:
1. Comment URL and time.
2. Classification: app, manual, expectation, missing context, or access/permission.
3. Exact reproduction steps.
4. Severity and affected route.
5. Owner and next verification.

### Launch-Day Control Board
| Toronto time | Physical action | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Launch moment | Place maker comment and inspect gallery/order | Correct copy and public route |
| Working-day start | Reply to every substantive comment | No unanswered question |
| Midday | Inspect conversion, errors and recurring objections | No secret or personal data in notes |
| Mid-afternoon | Share one factual learning if useful | No ranking/upvote request |
| Early evening | Reply and route beta requests | Consent path used |
| Product Hunt close | Capture results and unresolved faults | Backlog updated |

### Post-Launch Rail
```text
[Day 1] Reply + repair
   |
[Day 3] Publish verified fixes
   |
[Day 7] Compare visits, demo completions, confirmed beta joins and qualified conversations
   |
[Day 14] Decide what to keep, remove or rebuild
```

Measure facts, not leaderboard theatre:
- Product Hunt visits to the untracked primary link as reported by Product Hunt.
- `/try` completion events only after consent-safe analytics are configured.
- Confirmed Waitlister subscribers, not raw form submits.
- Qualified founder conversations and activated owners.
- Breakpoints classified and fixed.

### Safety Stickers
- [Platform] Share the launch link; never ask directly for upvotes.
- [Privacy] Route contact details through the consented intake, not public comments.
- [Evidence] Do not invent response times, customer counts, testimonials, rankings, or conversion rates.
- [Human] Every public response is reviewed and posted by the founder.

### Finished-Build Test

- [ ] Comment classification and evidence capture location are prepared.
- [ ] Every substantive comment receives a factual manual reply.
- [ ] Day-1, Day-3, Day-7 and Day-14 inspections occur.
