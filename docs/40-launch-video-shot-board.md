### Assembly Steps
#### Step 1 - Cut the 60-second launch film
Diagram:
```text
00:00 ├──────┬──────────┬──────────────┬──────────┤ 01:00
      HOOK   MECHANISM  PRODUCT PROOF  SAFE + CTA
      10     08          14 + 07        11 + 09
```
| Time | Place this frame | Motion jig | Founder line |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:06 | `10-no-circle-of-hell.png` | Hard cut; no orbiting decoration | “Your LinkedIn system should not trap you in a loop.” |
| 00:06–00:12 | `01-owner-sign-in.png` | Push from left to right; stop before the edge | “One private owner cabinet. No LinkedIn password.” |
| 00:12–00:24 | `08-try-the-mechanism.png` | Point to draft → clamp → queue | “Write the work. Test the voice. Queue only what passes.” |
| 00:24–00:38 | `14-product-showroom.png` | Step through the numbered product build | “Profile, drafts, voice, schedule, evidence, and manual—one assembly.” |
| 00:38–00:47 | `07-build-manual.png` | Hold on Place / Align / Avoid / Test | “Every feature ships with the panel that explains how to use it.” |
| 00:47–00:53 | `11-privacy.png` | Static hold; highlight owner control | “No scraping. No automated DMs. No machine editing your profile.” |
| 00:53–01:00 | `09-private-beta-waitlist.png` | Cut to yellow action; hold final two seconds | “Founder Above the Fold. Join the first fitting.” |

Check:
- The product is visible by second 12.
- The invitation is visible and readable for at least five seconds.
- No editor zoom cuts camera right.

Avoid:
- Do not use floating circles, talking-head bubbles, orbiting avatars, or circular masks.
- Do not show a live payment, real email, owner cabinet, or LinkedIn credential.

Check:
- Every claim points to a visible product part.
- Sandbox and setup-required states are described honestly.
- The voiceover never claims provider proof that is still red on the launch board.

Avoid:
- Do not call the product fully live, notarized, consent-configured, or LinkedIn-connected until those proof lights are green.

### Safety Stickers
- [Security] Use only the public screenshot crate.
- [Privacy] Replace any future entered address with a clearly fictional `.test` address.
- [Cost] Never film a completed checkout.
- [IP] Use supplied project typography and product captures; keep music/stock licensing evidence separately.
- [Evidence] Recapture after deployment so the film matches the shipped theme.
