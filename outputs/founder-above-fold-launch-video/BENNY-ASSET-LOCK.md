# Benny™ asset lock

## Provenance

Benny™ is the founder's original copyrighted and trademarked character, created from her visual memory of her childhood orange cat, Andy, and translated into a 1990s Saturday-morning-cartoon style.

## Permitted use

- Place the supplied transparent artwork as a locked foreground layer.
- Translate and uniformly scale the complete layer.
- Animate the complete layer with gentle position, scale, or rotation only when the entire artwork remains intact.

## Prohibited use

- Never generate, redraw, approximate, trace, recolour, restyle, re-costume, relight, inpaint, outpaint, facially alter, or reconstruct Benny™.
- Never crop or cover part of the supplied artwork to change its props or wording.
- Never show more than one Benny™ in a frame, including reflections, portraits, shadows, background cameos, or generated variants.
- Never use the cats generated inside the Whisk workshop clips as canonical character artwork.

## Scene strategy

Generate or draw the workshop without characters. Composite exactly one locked Benny™ master above the finished environment. Animate the machine independently beneath and beside him.
