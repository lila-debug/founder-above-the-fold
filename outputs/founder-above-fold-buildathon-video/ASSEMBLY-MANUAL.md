### Assembly Steps
#### Step 1 - Separate product pages from export debris
Diagram:
```text
[PDF pages 1–5] ---> [Film]
[Pitch advert 6] --X
```
Do:
1. Place pages one through five on the timeline.
2. Remove page six because it promotes the presentation tool rather than the product.

Check:
- Every deck claim in the film exists in the supplied PDF.

Avoid:
- Inventing prize eligibility, traction, customers, or a live publishing result.

#### Step 2 - Insert working-product proof
Diagram:
```text
[Product kit] ---> [Dashboard] ---> [Voice] ---> [Local AI]
```
Do:
1. Insert verified captures after the product-kit page.
2. Label them as real product surfaces.
3. Hold each long enough for a judge to read the headline and mechanism.

Check:
- The dashboard's zero automated-post state remains visible and truthful.

Avoid:
- Calling illustrative counts live customer evidence.

#### Step 3 - Lock and test the film
Diagram:
```text
[Lint] ---> [Snapshot] ---> [Render] ---> [Probe] ---> [Watch]
```
Do:
1. Run the composition checker.
2. Inspect one still from each visual beat.
3. Render the master and inspect its codecs, resolution, duration, and audio level.

Check:
- The MP4 plays for sixty seconds at 1920×1080 with audible narration.

Avoid:
- Calling the file finished if the PDF ad page appears or the final CTA is clipped.

### Safety Stickers
- [Security] No key, password, token, or private account appears.
- [Privacy] Voice capture is described exactly as the product screen states: recordings are not retained unless chosen.
- [Cost] The production uses the connected free plan and local rendering; no spend is authorised.
- [IP] The source deck and product captures are owner-provided. Pitch's advertisement page is excluded; its small source labels on exported pages are not erased.
- [Evidence] “Built in 21 straight hours” is a founder-provided fact. Prices and the 48-hour offer come directly from the supplied deck.
