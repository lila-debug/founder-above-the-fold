## Video direction

- The supplied PDF is the primary visual source. Pages 1–5 appear full bleed; page 6 is excluded because it is Pitch's export advertisement, not product content.
- Three verified product captures interrupt the deck so the film demonstrates a working app rather than becoming a narrated slideshow.
- Motion is a slow 1.00→1.035 camera settle with half-second cross-dissolves. Product labels snap in on a single axis and then hold.
- No invented customers, traction, eligibility, prize amount, analytics, or live LinkedIn state appears.
